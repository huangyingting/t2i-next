# Consensual BDSM/Kink Taxonomy for Image-Prompt Rules

> Status: Research note used to update the content-level rules.
>
> Scope: visually representable categories that sit between this project's
> **erotic** level (adult nudity and non-explicit intimacy, no explicit sexual
> acts or genital focus) and **hardcore** level (explicit consensual adult
> sexual acts). The goal is to give rule authors a stable taxonomy, per-category
> image-representability judgement, and a flagged exclusion list.
>
> Current mapping decision: BDSM is a theme, not a third content level. Its
> non-explicit, lower-risk visual forms belong to `erotic`; only independently
> visible explicit sexual activity selects `hardcore`. Restraint, equipment, or
> pain intensity never upgrades a scene by itself. The implemented rules use a
> deliberately narrower subset than the full taxonomy below.

## Consent Framework

All categories below apply only to **consensual adult activity**. Two dominant
community frameworks govern ethical BDSM practice:

- **SSC — Safe, Sane, Consensual**: activities must be physically safe, entered
  by participants of sound mind, and freely consented to. Adopted as community
  standard from the Gay Men's SM Activists (GMSMA) statement of purpose, circa
  1983. ([Wikipedia: Safe, sane and consensual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe,_sane_and_consensual))
- **RACK — Risk-Aware Consensual Kink**: emphasises individual responsibility
  and full disclosure of risk rather than a binary safe/unsafe distinction.
  ([Wikipedia: BDSM §Fundamental principles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BDSM))
- **EPP — Explicit Prior Permission**: a legal framework approved by the
  American Law Institute in 2021, requiring affirmative, prior consent to
  specific acts; explicitly excludes activities that risk serious injury such as
  breath-restriction play.
  ([NCSF: Consent Counts / EPP](https://ncsfreedom.org/key-programs-2/consent-counts/))

Consent in BDSM requires: **informed agreement, capacity to consent (no
intoxication), ongoing revocability (safeword), and negotiated scope.**
([Wikipedia: Consent (BDSM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consent_(BDSM)))

> **Image limitation**: a still image cannot establish consent, capacity, or
> negotiation. Rule authors must therefore mandate contextual markers — such as
> explicit play-space staging, props recognisable as consensual gear, or posed
> cooperative body language — and exclude content whose harm signal cannot be
> distinguished from non-consent.

## Includable Categories

The categories below are **visually representable** without requiring explicit
sexual acts or genital focus, and can be placed between erotic and hardcore
levels when adequately marked as consensual adult play.

### 1. Dominance & Submission (D/s)

**Definition**: a power-exchange dynamic in which one party (dominant/Dom/Domme)
takes psychological control over another (submissive/sub). Physical contact is
not required. ([Wikipedia: Dominance and submission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominance_and_submission))

**Visually representable elements**:
- Dominant commanding posture; submissive kneeling, bowing, or presenting pose
- Collars, leashes, or lead-chains used as ownership symbols
- Dominant holding a crop, flogger, or paddle as authority prop
- Public display on leash at a recognised play-space (e.g. Folsom Street Fair
  context) ([Wikipedia: Dominance and submission, image caption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominance_and_submission))
- Written or signed contracts displayed as scene props

**Placement**: spans erotic-bdsm-hardcore depending on whether explicit acts
appear; posture and gear alone are erotic-to-bdsm range.

### 2. Bondage & Restraint (B/D)

**Definition**: consensual tying, binding, or restraining of a partner for
erotic, aesthetic, or somatosensory stimulation.
([Wikipedia: Bondage (BDSM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondage_(BDSM)))

**Sub-types and visual markers**:

| Sub-type | Visual markers |
|---|---|
| Rope bondage / Shibari / Kinbaku | Decorative rope patterns on body, suspension rig |
| Breast bondage | Rope wrapped around torso/chest |
| Cuff restraints | Leather or metal wrist/ankle cuffs, bed restraints |
| Spreader bars | Rigid bar between limbs keeping them apart |
| St. Andrew's Cross (saltire) | X-shaped frame to which subject is secured |
| Mummification | Full-body wrapping in latex, cling-film, or bandages |
| Box tie / Ushiro takatekote | Arms bound behind back — common shibari form |
| Cage / confinement | Subject enclosed in purpose-built cage or kennel |

([Wikipedia: Bondage (BDSM) §Types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondage_(BDSM)))

**Placement**: bondage alone (no explicit acts) maps to erotic-bdsm range.
Bondage combined with explicit acts maps to bdsm-hardcore.

### 3. Sensory Deprivation

**Definition**: removal or restriction of one or more senses to heighten other
sensations or enforce submission. Common BDSM application uses blindfolds and
hoods; gags restrict speech.

**Visually representable elements**:
- Blindfold (cloth or leather)
- Hood (leather, latex, or fabric, covering head fully or partially)
- Ball gag, bit gag, or panel gag — restricts speech, visible on subject's face
- Bondage collar: restricts neck movement
- Earplugs or noise-cancelling headphones in a scene context

([Wikipedia: Bondage (BDSM), mentions blindfolds and gags](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondage_(BDSM));
[Wikipedia: BDSM §Behavioural aspects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BDSM))

**Placement**: sensory deprivation markers without explicit acts = erotic-bdsm
range. Gags alone are a clear bdsm marker.

### 4. Role Play / Costume Play

**Definition**: erotic scenarios in which participants adopt social or fantasy
roles to express power differentials or sexual fantasy. Identifiable by costume,
setting, and prop.
([Wikipedia: Sexual roleplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_roleplay))

**Common role pairs (visually distinguishable)**:
- Authority / subordinate: officer-prisoner, teacher-student, executive-secretary
- Service / power: dominatrix-client, mistress-slave (M/s)
- Fantasy / mythic: supernatural seducer-mortal, Gorean kajira scenario
- Gender-play: feminisation or cross-dressing within a D/s frame
- Uniform fetishism: French maid, leather officer, latex nurse

**Excluded role pairs** (see Exclusions section): any roleplay that simulates
age below adulthood, incest, or non-consensual abduction without clear
play-space markers.

([Wikipedia: Sexual roleplay §Examples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_roleplay))

**Placement**: costume/role markers alone = erotic-bdsm; explicit acts within
role play = bdsm-hardcore.

### 5. Sensation Play

**Definition**: deliberate application of varied sensory stimuli — from soft to
intense — to heighten arousal. Encompasses both gentle and pain-adjacent forms.
([Wikipedia: Sensation play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensation_play))

**Visually representable elements**:
- Feathers, silk scarves, fur gloves applied to skin
- Ice — visible cube or skin reaction (goosebumps, wetness)
- Wax play — candle and visible wax poured or set on skin
  ([Wikipedia: Master/slave (BDSM), image of wax play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master/slave_(BDSM)))
- Wartenberg pinwheel — spiked wheel drawn across skin, visible contact marks
- Clothespins or nipple clamps — visible clamp attached to skin fold
- Temperature contrasts (ice and warm wax in same scene)

**Placement**: gentle sensation play = erotic range; wax/clamps/wheel marks
visible = bdsm range; explicit genital stimulation = hardcore.

### 6. Impact / Pain Play

**Definition**: consensual striking of the body for erotic or disciplinary
stimulation. Implements range from open hand to specialised tools.
([Wikipedia: Impact play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_play))

**Visually representable elements**:

| Implement / act | Visual marker |
|---|---|
| Erotic spanking (hand) | Striking posture, reddened skin on buttocks |
| Paddle | Rectangular implement held by dominant |
| Flogger / multi-tail | Fan of leather strands, striking motion or marks |
| Cane / riding crop | Thin rigid implement; welt marks |
| Whip / single-tail | Long implement; red linear marks |
| Flogging at cross/post | Subject restrained at Saint Andrew's Cross |

([Wikipedia: Impact play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_play);
[Wikipedia: BDSM §Sensation play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BDSM))

**Placement**: implements without strikes = erotic-bdsm; visible marks
(redness, welts) without blood = bdsm range; blood or open wounds = excluded.

### 7. Protocol & Training Dynamics

**Definition**: structured rituals, postures, and rules that enact ongoing
authority exchange between dominant and submissive or Master and slave.
([Wikipedia: Master/slave (BDSM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master/slave_(BDSM)))

**Visually representable elements**:
- Collaring ceremony — dominant fastening a collar on a kneeling submissive
- Inspection / presentation postures (submissive presenting hands, kneeling)
- Slave training position (Nadu, display poses from Gorean lifestyle)
- Service ritual — submissive serving food/drink at dominant's feet
- Symbolic collar locked with padlock (marks M/s relationship)
- Slave register / contract as scene prop

([Wikipedia: Master/slave (BDSM) §Symbols and rituals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master/slave_(BDSM)))

**Placement**: protocol postures and collaring = erotic-bdsm range; no explicit
acts required.

### 8. Fetish Clothing & Equipment

**Definition**: specialised garments and accessories whose materials or design
carry erotic or power-exchange significance.
([Wikipedia: Fetish fashion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fetish_fashion))

**Material categories**: latex/rubber, leather, PVC, nylon/fishnet, spandex,
stainless steel hardware.

**Key garment / accessory types**:
- Full-body latex catsuit
- Leather or latex corset / bustier
- BDSM harness (body or chest harness in leather or rope)
- Collar (day collar, training collar, locking collar)
- Thigh-high boots (ballet boot, platform heel)
- Hobble skirt or restrictive skirt
- Dominatrix outfit (corset + boots + crop)
- Chastity device (cage or belt, visible as locked hardware)
- Bondage cuffs and restraint hardware

([Wikipedia: Fetish fashion §Types](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fetish_fashion))

**Placement**: clothing alone = erotic range; full gear in scene context =
bdsm range.

### 9. Consensual Erotic Humiliation (D/s context)

**Definition**: consensual psychological humiliation performed to produce sexual
arousal within a negotiated D/s relationship.
([Wikipedia: Erotic humiliation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erotic_humiliation))

**Visually representable elements**:
- Dominant commanding submissive to kneel or bow in a play space
- Submissive carrying dominant's belongings in service posture
- Leashed public parade within a recognised community event/venue
- Postures of servitude (shoe-cleaning, floor-level service)

**Critical image constraint**: humiliation imagery is **only includable** when
the scene is unambiguously staged as consensual play — through play-space
setting, costumes, visible community-event context, or cooperative body
language. Images where distress cannot be distinguished from genuine non-consent
must be excluded.

**Placement**: non-explicit humiliation postures = bdsm range; acts involving
bodily fluids = excluded (see Exclusions section).

## Excluded Categories

The following categories **must not appear** in any content level. The primary
reasons are: (a) a still image cannot establish consent or capacity; (b) the
depicted harm is serious or irreversible; (c) the category inherently involves
protected or vulnerable persons.

| Category | Reason for exclusion |
|---|---|
| **Minors / age-ambiguous subjects** | Absolute legal and ethical prohibition. Images cannot verify age. All subjects must be unambiguously adult. |
| **Ageplay depicting minor-coded dynamics** | Even between adults, simulation of sexual dynamics with a minor-presenting role normalises harm and is unrepresentable without establishing consent context an image cannot provide. ([Wikipedia: Sexual roleplay §Ageplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_roleplay)) |
| **Breath play / Erotic asphyxiation / Choking** | Leading cause of death in BDSM-related fatalities (Schori et al. 2022, cited by NCSF). Illegal in most U.S. states regardless of consent. EPP explicitly excludes it. ([NCSF: Is Erotic Choking Legal?](https://ncsfreedom.org/2023/04/22/is-erotic-choking-legal/)) |
| **Blood / Cutting / Scarification / Open wounds** | Serious physical injury; no image can establish informed consent to lasting harm. Impact marks (redness, welts) short of blood are in bdsm range; blood is not. |
| **Weapons (real edged or firearms)** | Weapons in a sexual context imply coercion or lethal threat; an image cannot establish that a real weapon is an inert prop. |
| **Intoxication / Incapacitation** | An incapacitated person cannot consent. Images showing alcohol, drug use, or incapacitation in a sexual context are excluded at every level. ([Wikipedia: Consent (BDSM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consent_(BDSM))) |
| **Non-consensual appearance / Coercion** | Humiliation or distress that is visually indistinguishable from genuine non-consent is excluded. Only unambiguously staged play-space scenes are includable. |
| **Incest fantasy roleplay** | Too high a risk of harmful normalisation; cannot be safely distinguished from actual incest depiction. |
| **Animals in sexual context** | Sexual content involving actual animals is excluded absolutely. Human animal-roleplay costumes (pony play, pet play) between consenting adults are distinct and are in the role-play category above, provided no actual animal is present or implied. |
| **Bodily-fluid humiliation (urolagnia / coprophilia)** | Watersports and scat exceed the bdsm visual range, appear on most platform exclusion lists, and cannot establish consent context in image form. ([Wikipedia: Erotic humiliation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erotic_humiliation)) |
| **Uncontrolled electrical play** | Uncontrolled electrical stimulation risks cardiac arrest; indistinguishable from torture in a still image. |
| **Sleeping / unconscious subjects in sexual context** | No image can establish consent from an incapacitated or sleeping subject. |
