BRIEF

Create emotionally exact object-biography Themes where one ordinary object
reveals its history through visible repairs, wear, modifications, and present
use. The requested adult cast must be engaged with the object's current function,
not posing beside sentimental memorabilia.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
actively using the biographical object. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, clearly visible face and body, position, gaze, and functional hand
action. A former owner in a portrait, silhouette, reflection, or off-camera
presence does not count. Additional CLI-requested adults may participate, but
she must remain integral to the object's present use. This brief is incompatible
with a zero-woman cast.

BIOGRAPHY THROUGH MATERIAL

Choose a different non-famous, flat-fronted object for each Theme. Give it three
chronologically compatible traces selected from manufacture, sustained use,
repair, and adaptation. Every trace must occupy a specific material location and have a
credible cause. Do not invent inscriptions, owners, wars, celebrity provenance,
or tragedy to manufacture importance.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, era, exact site, adult cast, object, three traces,
and current use. The
style sentence defines observational photography, color, lens, light, and the
balance between human scale and material close detail.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame shows the whole object, requested cast, all three selected traces,
and one current action in a single exposure. Describe each trace's location, texture,
edge behavior, discoloration or deformation, and relationship to present use.
The cast's hands, weight, tools, and eyelines must make the object's function
clear. Place all three traces on the same broad front surface. Use an ordinary
frontal or slight three-quarter camera angle that keeps them visible. Avoid
edge, hinge, deeply recessed, interior, or underside traces, and do not place a
trace behind the cast.

Preserve object dimensions and trace continuity across Frames while varying the
side viewed, active function, and which trace controls the composition. Use no
labels, museum vitrines, floating detail insets, ghost owners, flashbacks,
split-screen history, or explanatory documents.

Reject pristine antiques, generic patina, damage without cause, and Frames where
the prose tells a biography that the surface cannot visibly support.
