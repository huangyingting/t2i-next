BRIEF

Create extraordinary botanical time-slice photographs in which several growth
stages of one plant coexist as a single physically staged image. The image is not
a time-lapse strip: one continuous environment must hold a readable progression
from an early state to a late state.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
actively tending, studying, harvesting, protecting, or navigating the plant
system. Give her a specific adult age, stable appearance, position, gaze, and
necessary action. A silhouette, portrait, reflection, or off-camera presence
does not count. Include additional CLI-requested adults without reducing her to
decoration. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

GROWTH LOGIC

Choose a botanically credible plant and a different growth event for each Theme:
germination, unfurling, flowering, pollination, fruiting, seed release, seasonal
senescence, or recovery after weather. Arrange stages through radial beds,
terraced soil, graft-like scenic construction, concentric greenhouse zones, or
another practical device. Never place multiple stages on one impossible stem.

The exact requested adult cast tends, studies, harvests, protects, or navigates
the living progression. Their action must clarify scale and direction without
turning them into static presenters. Do not add other people.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names the country, season, site, species or credible plant family,
the visible biological transition, the practical staging logic, and the cast's
immediate purpose. The style defines medium, color behavior, moisture and surface
rendering, and one dominant light condition.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame shows the entire time-slice system in one exposure from a new
composition. Describe where the youngest and oldest states sit, how intermediate
states connect spatially, and one cast action that affects exactly one zone.
Include truthful roots, stems, leaves, flowers or fruit, soil contact, gravity,
weather response, and scale. Preserve the same cast and core plant system across
Frames while changing task, camera, and the locally emphasized growth stage.

Use no labels, calendars, arrows, floating specimens, scientific UI, split
screens, or magical glowing transitions. Reject six repeated potted plants, a
normal garden with an unsupported claim of time, and any biological sequence
whose stages or season contradict one another.
