BRIEF

Create tactile material-alchemy Themes where one flexible mundane material
occupies three physically credible deformation states inside one photographic scene.
The transformation must feel astonishing because its real behavior is shown
precisely, not because magic words are applied to it.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
actively operating or controlling the deformation process. Give her a specific
adult age, stable appearance, clearly visible face and body, safe support,
assigned hands, gaze, and necessary action. A silhouette, portrait, reflection,
or off-camera operator does not count. Additional CLI-requested adults may help,
but she must remain essential to the material change. This brief is incompatible
with a zero-woman cast.

TRANSFORMATION RULE

Choose a different safe, flexible material and one visible mechanical press,
roller, clamp, tensioning frame, or weighted platen for every Theme. Show
untouched material, a zone actively deformed by the device, and a connected zone
recovering after release. They belong to one sheet, rope, foam block, fiber bed,
or continuous batch. Do not use heat, chemistry, drying, or aging.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, exact working site, adult cast, material, mechanical
device, three adjoining deformation states, practical purpose, and one
decision at a transition boundary. The style sentence defines macro-to-environment detail, palette,
surface response, light, and capture medium.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame contains the same three deformation states within one continuous
batch on one support. Make the transition direction and device contact clear. Name the shared support, mechanical device,
and one current cast action affecting only the active deformation zone.
Describe direct consequences such as condensation, cracking, stretching,
bubbling, staining, granulation, or residue where appropriate.

Protect anatomy and safety: assign every hand, tool, barrier, and support;
separate people from dangerous temperatures or pressure; and keep fluids and
particles governed by gravity and airflow. No levitation, fantasy glow, unlabeled
laboratory UI, split screens, or unexplained completed products.

Reject three unrelated samples, decorative smoke,
or any transformation where the energy path and material consequence are not
both visible.
