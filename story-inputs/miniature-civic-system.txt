BRIEF

Create high-concept miniature civic-system Themes in which the exact requested
adult cast operates a tiny but functional public service built from familiar
household or natural materials. The image must reveal a coherent system, not
merely tiny people exploring a giant object.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
as a clearly visible operator of the miniature civic system. Give her a specific
adult age, stable appearance, grounded position, gaze, and necessary service
task. A tiny anonymous citizen, silhouette, portrait, reflection, or off-camera
operator does not count. Additional requested adults may operate other
links, but she must remain indispensable. This brief is incompatible with a
zero-woman cast.

SYSTEM DESIGN

Invent a different service for each Theme: transport, water delivery, shade,
communication, repair, food exchange, waste sorting, flood control, public
performance, or another constructive shared function. Build source, route,
transfer point, destination, and feedback from the chosen material ecology.
The cast remains normal adults within the declared miniature world; do not add
anonymous citizens.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, miniature site, adult cast, service, source material,
five-part system, and one operational problem requiring a visible choice. The
style sentence defines practical macro photography, scale anchors, palette,
texture, and light.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame is a standalone operating state of the same civic system. Trace the
service from source to destination, assign every person a distinct necessary
task, and show one material signal proving whether the system currently works.
Tools and structures must derive from the hero material rather than arbitrary
miniature machinery.

Use believable joints, loads, channels, traction, fluid paths, supports, and
maintenance access. Include at least three unmistakable scale anchors. Avoid
toy-town styling, giant human hands from another scale, crowds, signage, labels,
or whimsical architecture with no function.

Reject Themes reducible to climbing, bathing, sheltering, or gazing; systems
without feedback; and Frames where removing the cast would leave the same image.
