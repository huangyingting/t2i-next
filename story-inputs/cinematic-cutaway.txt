BRIEF

Create cinematic architectural cutaway Themes in which a single building section
reveals the requested adult cast and the hidden system shaping their immediate
situation. Treat the cutaway as a convincing practical miniature or full-scale
set, not a software diagram.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
inside the occupied action zone. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, clearly visible body, position, gaze, and necessary response to the
hidden system. A miniature anonymous worker, silhouette, portrait, or off-camera
occupant does not count. Include additional CLI-requested adults without
weakening her role. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

SECTION LOGIC

Each Theme chooses a different building type and one concealed system: water,
heat, ventilation, roots, sound, mechanical power, storage, light, or circulation.
The cut plane must expose rooms, wall thickness, floor layers, and the selected
system while leaving a structurally credible building. The system creates one
visible consequence in the occupied room.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise states country, era, building function, cast, hidden system, current
consequence, and why the cast must respond now. The style sentence defines the
practical-model aesthetic, material detail, scale cues, palette, and light.

FRAME CONTRACT

Every Frame is one oblique sectional view containing one coherent building, not
multiple panels. Identify the cut plane, visible rooms, exposed construction
layers, route of the hidden system, and occupied action zone. The exact cast must
remain full-size people within the chosen visual logic; do not add tiny workers,
bystanders, portraits, or silhouettes.

Show a complete causal chain: source, route, obstruction or transfer, and visible
effect. Keep pipes connected, beams supported, stairs landable, rooms accessible,
water governed by gravity, and light consistent across interior and exterior.
Use a moderate axonometric or three-quarter camera without impossible perspective.

No labels, legends, exploded parts, blueprint overlays, dollhouse facade, or
transparent walls. Reject a cutaway that merely displays rooms, a hidden system
with no effect on the cast, or a dramatic event unsupported by the exposed
mechanism.
