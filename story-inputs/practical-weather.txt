BRIEF

Create spectacular practical-weather Themes staged with real controllable
effects around the exact requested adult cast. Weather must transform one
specific environment and human task while remaining physically safe, coherent,
and photographable.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
actively responding to the practical weather effect. Give her a specific adult
age, stable appearance, sharp face and body silhouette, grounded support, gaze,
and necessary task. A blurred silhouette, portrait, reflection, or off-camera
crew member does not count. Additional CLI-requested adults may appear, but she
must remain central and unobscured by weather. This brief is incompatible with
a zero-woman cast.

WEATHER MACHINE

For each Theme choose one dominant effect: rain, wind, snow substitute, ground
fog, heat shimmer, drifting ash substitute, dust, condensation, or spray.
Define a visible or inferable practical source, travel direction, obstacles,
accumulation zones, and shutdown boundary. Combine secondary effects only when
they naturally follow from the dominant one.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, controlled location, adult cast, weather effect,
source and direction, current task, material obstacle, and the decisive response.
The style sentence defines shutter behavior, palette, backlight or sidelight,
surface detail, and production scale.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame freezes one current weather state. Describe source, vector, interaction
with architecture, response of hair and clothing, ground or surface accumulation,
and one cast action with stable support. Keep faces, body silhouettes, hands,
task, and footing sharp and legible even when particles or droplets carry motion.

Use drainage, airflow, splash, fabric weight, visibility, footprints, wetness,
temperature cues, and gravity consistently. Place machinery outside body paths;
no dangerous debris, lightning strikes, uncontrolled fire, flooding, falls,
panic, injury, or impossible indoor storms.

Reject weather as a background overlay, motion blur without a source, dry
surfaces under heavy rain, wind acting in contradictory directions, or spectacle
that leaves the cast with nothing necessary to do.
