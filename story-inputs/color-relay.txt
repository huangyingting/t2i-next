BRIEF

Create sophisticated color-relay Themes where one saturated color travels through
a chain of real materials and ends by changing the requested adult cast's
immediate decision. Color is a physical participant carried by pigment, dyed
fabric, projected light, reflected light, liquid, powder, glass, smoke, or
another credible medium.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
at the final link of the color relay. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, clearly visible face and body, position, gaze, and consequential
action. A silhouette, reflection, portrait, or implied recipient does not count.
Additional CLI-requested adults may appear, but she must remain essential to the
relay's final decision. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

RELAY RULE

Design a chain of exactly four distinct carriers. Each carrier must receive the
color from the previous one through visible contact, reflection, transmission,
spillage, staining, casting, or shadow. The hue may shift predictably through
mixing or filtering, but it cannot teleport or appear independently.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names the country, exact site, cast, starting source, four-carrier
chain, final human consequence, and one emotionally legible choice. The style
sentence defines the neutral counter-palette, capture medium, texture, and
exposure strategy that protects the color chain.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame shows the complete four-link relay in one continuous composition.
Describe every carrier's physical location, transfer boundary, surface response,
and order. Give the cast one current action at the final link and keep their
clothing, skin, and surroundings free of unexplained copies of the relay color.

Use realistic absorption, gloss, translucency, scattering, fluid direction,
shadow, and color contamination. Compose the relay as a readable path through
foreground, action plane, and background without arrows or labels. Preserve
natural skin color and avoid clipped channels.

FINAL GATE

Across Themes change the color, transfer physics, setting, scale, route shape,
and human decision. Reject monochrome set dressing, four unrelated objects of
the same hue, neon ambience without transfer, or any chain whose middle link can
be removed without changing the image.
