BRIEF

Create transporting threshold-world Themes centered on one real doorway,
window, gate, curtain, hatch, arch, or natural opening that separates two
materially distinct but geographically connected environments. The requested
adult cast must make one visible decision at the boundary.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
actively opening, closing, crossing, passing, blocking, admitting, sealing, or
holding the threshold. Give her a specific adult age, stable appearance,
clearly visible face and body, support, gaze, and boundary action. A silhouette,
portrait, reflection, or off-camera traveler does not count. Additional
CLI-requested adults may appear, but she must remain essential to the boundary
decision. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

BOUNDARY LOGIC

For each Theme choose two spaces that can credibly touch. Contrast them through
function, climate, light, sound-implying materials, social use, or degree of
maintenance without resorting to fantasy portals. The threshold has thickness,
hardware, wear, and a practical way to cross. Environmental effects may pass
through only according to airflow, drainage, light, and temperature.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, era, both exact spaces, adult cast, threshold
construction, environmental contrast, and one visible boundary action or
committed hold. Use an observable verb such as opening, closing, crossing,
passing, blocking, admitting, sealing, or holding open; thinking, considering,
hesitating, deciding, or resting a hand does not count. The style
sentence defines exposure balance, palette relationship, medium, lens, and
texture.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame shows both spaces and the entire crossing geometry in one exposure.
Describe near side, threshold plane, far side, cast positions, support, gaze,
and repeat the observable action or forceful hold occurring exactly at the
boundary. If a door or gate is moving or held open, show it between 30 and 60
degrees open, with the cast visibly applying force and with clearance from walls
and stops.
Establish which light belongs
to each side and how it spills, reflects, or is blocked.

Keep floor levels, wall thickness, doors, hinges, steps, weather, clothing, and
perspective coherent. No magical glow, teleportation, impossible seasonal
collision, floating doors, split screens, labels, or a horizon that changes
across the opening.

VARIETY AND REJECTION

Vary threshold type, paired functions, direction of view, era, weather, decision,
and emotional dynamic. Reject a person simply standing in a doorway, two spaces
whose contrast is only color grading, or a boundary that cannot physically
connect the described environments.
