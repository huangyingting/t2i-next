BRIEF

Create polished, varied lifestyle photo stories inspired by the visual language
of Instagram and Xiaohongshu. Each Theme presents one believable adult social
moment with attractive styling, a specific real-world place, an activity worth
sharing, and the approachable finish of a carefully made social-feed image.

This is photography for an imagined lifestyle post or carousel, not a literal
screen capture of either application. Never render an app interface, profile
page, username, caption box, hashtag, like count, comment, carousel dot,
notification, device frame, platform logo, watermark, QR code, or invented
readable text.

PLATFORM-INSPIRED VISUAL DIRECTIONS

Choose one direction for each Theme and let it shape location, styling,
activity, framing, light, palette, and surface finish.

INSTAGRAM EDITORIAL

- aspirational but believable lifestyle photography with a strong hero image;
- confident composition, selective depth, rich natural color, and a cohesive
  editorial grade;
- expressive fashion, travel, dining, architecture, wellness, beauty, or
  creative-work settings;
- sunset warmth, window light, flash-at-dusk, hard urban sun, gallery-soft
  daylight, or restrained nightlife color;
- polished spontaneity: the image feels caught in a real moment even when the
  wardrobe, location, and light are carefully coordinated; and
- global visual fluency without copying a real creator, campaign, or post.

XIAOHONGSHU LIFESTYLE

- bright, clean, useful-looking lifestyle imagery with intimate diary energy;
- airy daylight, fresh skin, gentle color, clear materials, and readable
  everyday details;
- outfit-of-the-day, cafe visit, city walk, weekend trip, home routine, beauty
  preparation, food discovery, shopping find, fitness, hobby, or workspace;
- an inviting sense that the viewer could visit the place, try the styling, or
  recreate the activity;
- careful attention to garments, accessories, makeup, food, interiors,
  packaging shape, and tactile objects without turning the image into a catalog;
  and
- friendly refinement without childish posing, artificial cuteness, excessive
  whitening, plastic skin, or a generic beauty-filter face.

HYBRID SOCIAL EDITORIAL

Blend Instagram's stronger hero composition and color confidence with
Xiaohongshu's bright intimacy, practical detail, and everyday accessibility.
The result should feel personally authored rather than corporate, but more
intentional than an accidental snapshot.

CAST AND IDENTITY

Use exactly the requested number of adult women and adult men. Add no
bystanders, staff, crowds, reflected strangers, screen figures, posters,
mannequins, dolls, or disembodied hands that resemble extra people.

Every person is unmistakably twenty-five or older. Give each Theme distinct
fictional adults with exact ages, facial structures, builds, skin tones,
hairstyles, and personal styling. Unless the Story Description explicitly
specifies another nationality, every woman is Chinese and every Frame must
identify her naturally as a Chinese woman. Apply the same Chinese nationality
default independently to every man. Never infer another nationality from a
foreign-inspired outfit, activity, architecture, food, name, or visual style.
Preserve each identity across all Frames in that Theme. Do not name, imitate,
or closely resemble a real influencer, celebrity, model, public figure, or
private person.

Unless the Story Description explicitly identifies another country, set every
Theme in China and name a credible Chinese city, district, landscape, or
interior context. Vary regions and city character across Themes without
changing the cast's default Chinese nationality.

With multiple people, make their relationship visually legible through mutual
gaze, shared activity, coordinated movement, exchanged objects, or natural
physical proximity. Do not reduce companions to blurred background decoration.

SHAREABLE STORY OCCASIONS

Choose or invent one concrete occasion per Theme. Suitable directions include:

- outfit-of-the-day before brunch, an exhibition, a concert, a date, work, or
  an evening event;
- discovering a visually distinctive cafe, bakery, restaurant, tea room,
  market stall, or rooftop;
- a city walk through an arcade, old neighborhood, waterfront, design
  district, garden, bookstore, or architectural landmark;
- a weekend hotel, countryside escape, beach morning, mountain lodge, train
  journey, road stop, or culturally specific local trip;
- a home morning, cooking session, reading corner, flower arrangement,
  record-listening ritual, pet-free quiet evening, or tidy workspace;
- skincare, makeup, hair styling, fragrance, jewelry, manicure, or wardrobe
  preparation shown as an adult routine;
- pottery, painting, photography, music, sewing, dance practice, gardening,
  collecting, journaling, or another visually active hobby;
- yoga, pilates, running, cycling, tennis, swimming, hiking, or a calm
  post-workout moment;
- tasting one specific dish or drink with believable serving ware, texture,
  steam, condensation, crumbs, or other fresh physical evidence; or
- a friendship, couple, or small-group moment organized around one shared
  place and activity.

Avoid generic posing in an unspecified luxury room. The location and activity
must provide concrete evidence of why this moment is worth sharing.

SETTING AND MATERIAL DETAIL

Use real, coherent locations with enough detail to feel visitable but not so
much clutter that the people disappear. Vary independent cafes, apartments,
hotel rooms, streets, gardens, galleries, studios, shops, transit spaces,
restaurants, coastlines, countryside, cultural interiors, and contemporary
public architecture.

Describe a few identifying materials and objects: tile, plaster, timber,
stainless steel, linen, glass, stone, ceramics, paper, flowers, food, bags,
books, sports equipment, beauty tools, or travel items. Every object should
support the activity, styling, palette, or composition. Avoid random luxury
props, implausible product piles, and sterile showroom perfection.

FASHION, BEAUTY, AND GROOMING

Give every person a complete, occasion-appropriate look. Name garment pieces,
silhouette, layers, colors, materials, footwear, bag, jewelry, eyewear,
headwear, and other visible accessories as relevant. Vary relaxed tailoring,
soft knitwear, dresses, denim, activewear, vacation layers, evening looks,
streetwear, vintage pieces, minimalist basics, and locally appropriate
clothing instead of repeating one influencer uniform.

For every woman, describe a coherent adult makeup direction with complexion
finish, blush, brows, eye treatment, lashes, and lips. For every man, describe
hair, facial hair or clean shave, complexion texture, and camera-ready
grooming. Preserve pores, fine hair, fabric pressure, mild asymmetry, and
natural skin variation. Beauty may be polished but never waxy, doll-like,
heavily filtered, or anatomically altered.

If a product is central to the moment, keep it fictional and unbranded unless
the Story Description explicitly requires visible text. Describe shape,
material, color, scale, handling, and relationship to the activity rather than
inventing a logo or promotional claim.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION

Make every Frame a single full-bleed photograph. Use portrait-oriented social
photography unless the Story Description requests another format. Do not
create a collage, moodboard, contact sheet, phone screenshot, multi-panel page,
advertisement layout, or visible border.

Choose a capture method that belongs to the moment:

- nearby-friend handheld portrait with relaxed eye contact;
- companion-taken candid while the subject performs the activity;
- arm's-length selfie with credible phone distance and perspective;
- mirror selfie with one physically coherent reflection and no extra people;
- timer or fixed-camera full-body outfit portrait;
- environmental portrait showing person and destination together;
- seated table portrait with food, drink, book, or creative work;
- over-the-shoulder activity view;
- low or high oblique fashion angle;
- walking, doorway, elevator, staircase, window, or street-crossing portrait;
- close beauty detail of face, hair, jewelry, hands, or makeup; or
- clean tabletop or object detail only when the requested cast remains visibly
  present elsewhere in that same single photograph.

Across a batch, vary full-body, three-quarter, waist-up, close portrait,
environmental-wide, tabletop-adjacent, mirror, and movement-led framing.
Avoid repeating centered eye-level posing, the same cafe table, the same
mirror selfie, or the same hand touching hair.

Use credible modern phone-camera or compact-camera optics: natural perspective,
controlled highlight roll-off, realistic HDR, moderate depth of field, clean
focus on eyes or the active object, and plausible edge detail. Optional direct
flash may feel deliberate and stylish. Avoid extreme fisheye distortion,
fake miniature blur, excessive bokeh, crunchy sharpening, blown highlights,
muddy shadows, or synthetic cinematic teal-orange grading.

LIGHT AND COLOR

Motivate light from the location: large windows, open shade, direct morning or
late-afternoon sun, overcast sky, practical cafe bulbs, bathroom mirror light,
store lighting, sunset, street lamps, or tasteful direct flash. State light
direction and how it affects skin, fabric, food, glass, metal, and the room.

Build one coherent palette from the outfit and setting. Suitable directions
include milky neutrals with one fruit color, warm timber and cream, clean blue
and white, sage and stone, cherry red with charcoal, butter yellow with denim,
muted rose with cocoa, coastal cyan with sand, or an original locally
motivated palette. Keep whites natural and skin tones believable.

AUTHENTICITY

Include small signs of lived reality: a shifted sleeve, wind-touched hair,
condensation on a glass, a half-open tote, creased linen, moving curtain,
sunlight crossing the floor, crumbs beside a pastry, a damp umbrella, or a
shopping receipt turned face down. Use only details that support the moment.

Do not make every scene impossibly luxurious, perfectly empty, or aggressively
staged. Do not use fake candid laughter without a cause. Expressions should
respond to the activity: concentration, quiet pleasure, curiosity, amusement,
conversation, appetite, post-exercise calm, or direct self-possessed posing.

CONTENT LEVEL

Follow the requested content level while retaining the same polished
social-lifestyle photographic language.

- Aesthetic: public-facing adult lifestyle imagery with complete opaque or
  tastefully sensual styling and no explicit sexual action.
- Erotic: private adult lifestyle-glamour or boudoir imagery with partial
  nudity, open or sheer garments, intimate self-touch, or reciprocal
  non-explicit contact. Do not imply that it is suitable for public posting.
- Hardcore: private explicit adult creator photography with requested
  consensual action clearly visible, while wardrobe, makeup, location,
  personal objects, social-photo framing, and polished light remain
  intentional. Do not render an application interface or claim platform
  approval.

Every person remains awake, willing, responsive, and able to stop. Exclude
minors, youthful ambiguity, coercion, intoxication, injury, hidden recording,
voyeurism, or non-consensual exposure.

THEME CONTRACT

Every Theme defines:

- one platform-inspired direction: Instagram editorial, Xiaohongshu lifestyle,
  or a deliberate hybrid;
- one concrete occasion, location, activity, and shareable visual hook;
- the exact requested cast with stable adult identities and relationships;
- one complete wardrobe, accessory, makeup, and grooming system;
- one capture method, framing family, light design, and color palette;
- a few activity-bearing objects and lived-in details; and
- a distinct identity from nearby Themes.

Themes should differ through occasion, place, activity, styling, capture
method, palette, mood, and social perspective rather than merely replacing one
prop or background color.

FRAME CONTRACT

Multiple Frames within one Theme form a visually coherent social carousel, but
they are parallel finished alternatives rather than chronological steps. Each
Frame must independently restate the full cast, identities, styling, location,
activity, camera method, light, and all visible content required to render it.
Never refer to another Frame, a previous image, or what happened before.

Vary framing, gesture, gaze, body orientation, activity detail, foreground
layer, and focus hierarchy while preserving the Theme's people, relationship,
occasion, wardrobe system, location, palette, and time window.

OUTPUT

Write every final Frame as one fluent natural-language paragraph in the
requested language, directly usable as an image-generation prompt. Begin with
the visible person, activity, and place rather than a title, platform name, or
rule explanation. Integrate styling, expression, pose, objects, composition,
capture method, light, color, and material detail in natural visual order.

The words Instagram and Xiaohongshu are invisible art direction only. Do not
render or describe their interfaces, trademarks, logos, captions, handles,
hashtags, engagement counts, or watermarks inside the image. Output no
headings, labels, bullet points, alternatives, implementation notes, or
compliance explanations.

Never write the words Instagram or Xiaohongshu in a Theme title, premise,
style, or final Frame. Translate the selected direction into visible
photography, styling, activity, light, color, and material language instead.
