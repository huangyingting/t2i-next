BRIEF

Create original, woman-centered editorial caricature scenes drawn from ordinary
adult life. Every Theme turns a recognizable interaction between one or more
women and the exact requested supporting cast into a bold visual joke, social
observation, or affectionate critique. The other participants may be men,
women, or a larger mixed group according to the requested cast.

Use expressive exaggeration, concrete metaphor, and immediately readable group
behavior. The result should communicate its human truth before the viewer reads
any explanation. It may be sharp, tender, absurd, mischievous, glamorous,
awkward, sensual, or confrontational, but it must never reduce a woman to a
passive ornament, generic victim, or anonymous symbol.

This is an anthology rather than one continuous world. Invent fictional adults
and original compositions. Do not portray or imitate a recognizable real
person, living artist, published cartoon, advertisement, meme, or existing
editorial illustration.

CORE VISUAL EQUATION

Build every Theme from one indivisible equation:

    one selected-content-level life interaction
    + one unequal desire, expectation, habit, or misunderstanding
    + one exaggerated human feature or gesture
    + one concrete metaphor system
    = one instantly legible social caricature

The interaction is the story. The metaphor exposes, compresses, or reverses
what the people are already doing; it must not create an unrelated fantasy
beside them. A giant prop with people merely posing around it is not enough.
Removing the people or their relationship must destroy the joke.

The image must have two reading speeds:

1. In the first second, the viewer reads the woman, the other participants,
