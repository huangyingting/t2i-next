BRIEF

Create varied, historically grounded Tang-dynasty Chinese figure-painting
Themes in the visual language of anonymous High-to-Middle Tang silk 人物画:
court ladies, officials, scholars, musicians, and private household adults
rendered as 绢本重彩, not as photography, live action, mural facsimile,
Dunhuang cave art, later-dynasty painting, or modern illustration. Combine
mature adult presence, iron-wire line, mineral 平涂, painterly 渲染, plausible
Tang dress, hair, ornaments, body, and makeup, outing and household space,
and the material presence of an old silk painting. Every
result must be an original anonymous-workshop composition, not a copy,
variation, signature style, or attributed work by an identifiable artist.

The result is a painted historical silk panel or handscroll segment on
prepared silk. It is not live action, photography, a film still, Western oil
painting, Japanese ukiyo-e, Song landscape, Ming gongbi album-leaf erotica,
Qing court painting, modern hanfu photography, anime, CGI, game art, a museum
display, or a photograph of framed artwork.

PERIOD AND CLASS REGISTER

Keep one period register per Theme so body, brow, bun, makeup, and textile
weight agree:

- Early Tang: a slightly slimmer adult body than High Tang, longer narrow
  brows, modest 花钿, lower piled hair, more 交领 or closer necklines, and
  lighter gauze;
- High Tang: the default court-lady canon of this brief: ample body, 阔眉 or
  倒晕眉, 开额, 义髻, 簪花, generous neckline above a 齐胸裙, and heavy
  mineral color;
- Middle to Late Tang: still adult and ample, with more tilted 堕马髻 or
  towering piles, optional 时世妆 of paler powder and darker 乌膏 lips, and
  more agitated 披帛.

Do not mix a High Tang flowered 义髻 with Song slenderness, or Late Tang
乌唇 with Early Tang Sui-like slimness, inside one Theme.

Give each adult a stable social class and dress her or him down or up to
match. A noble or 命妇 may wear 大袖衫, 簪花, 步摇, and 臂钏. A household
wife uses fewer pins, a simpler 高髻, and quieter damask. An adult attendant,
when she is part of the requested cast, uses a lower bun or mature 双鬟, a
plain 披帛, and little or no 花钿. Do not costume every woman as the same
flower-crowned court lady.

HAIRSTYLES

Give every adult a stable, historically adult Tang hairstyle and keep it
identical across Frames of the same Theme.
