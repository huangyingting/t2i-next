BRIEF

Create elegant negative-space Themes in which the empty area between the
requested adult cast and real objects forms a second immediately recognizable
image. Both the primary scene and the void image must carry meaning; neither may
be a decorative silhouette.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
whose clearly visible body contour helps form the negative space while she
performs a useful primary action. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, grounded position, gaze, and action. A shadow, silhouette alone,
portrait, reflection, or off-camera body does not count. Additional
CLI-requested adults may appear without replacing her contour and primary role.
This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

VOID CONSTRUCTION

For each Theme choose a distinct primary activity, object arrangement, and one
simple void image: a triangle, diamond, crescent, leaf, sail, or arrowhead.
Construct one recognizable void from a body contour and nearby edges of one
large architectural opening or piece of furniture. Choose a simple shape whose
straight or curved outline already suits those contours; do not combine several
distant objects or use a symbol needing internal details. Keep the boundaries
at similar depth so the camera alignment remains simple.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, exact location, adult cast, primary action, void
image, boundary elements, and the contradiction or emotional relation between
the two readings. The style sentence defines graphic contrast, medium, palette,
focus, and light direction.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame is one full-bleed composition from a single camera position. Make
that form unambiguous in the natural prose, then describe the useful primary
action and identify the nearby physical contours and
the resolved secondary image. Do not use shadows as
boundaries. Give each person stable support, natural
anatomy, one action, and one gaze target independent of the void illusion.

Keep the void genuinely empty except for uniform atmosphere; no painted fill,
projected picture, smoke illustration, digital mask, typography, or birds placed
to complete the shape. Preserve subtle depth cues while aligning the contours.
The secondary image may be symbolic but must be concrete and nameable.

Use triangle, diamond, or arrowhead when boundaries are straight; use crescent
or leaf only when the physical boundaries are genuinely curved. Reject random
gaps, hearts or keyholes, complex symbols, shapes assembled from
incompatible straight and curved boundaries, silhouettes requiring a
caption, frozen posing without a useful primary action, or compositions where
the primary activity exists only to force the outline.
