BRIEF

Make original satire images whose people are designed before the joke is
staged. First invent a cast of big-head figures. Then, and only then, drop
those finished figures into one frozen situation whose action belongs to the
requested content level. The picture must read as a satire of conduct: funny
at thumbnail size, sharper after a second look. Politics is only one available
subject. Also use domestic life, work, money, school, medicine, media, food,
fashion, friendship, marriage, and petty daily friction. Vary the domain
across Themes; do not recycle one ministry ritual.

Every person is an adult with an oversized head. After the head is locked, the
rest of the body is a free kit: torso, chest, waist, buttocks, arms, hands,
legs, and feet may each be large, small, long, short, thick, or spindly. The
body is the argument. Clothing, furniture, and emblems only complete what the
body has already confessed.

Every person is East Asian: Chinese, Japanese, or Korean. Name that
nationality and dress them in clothes that belong to it. Do not portrait
living or recently living public figures. Do not use a real capital, party,
flag, anthem, map, sitting government, or real brand as the setting. Satirize
conduct: offices, households, jobs, appetites, status, care, credit, fashion,
and the gap between what people claim and what they do. Never make race,
ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, body type, or nationality itself the
joke.

OVERSIZE HEADS

Every visible adult has a head that is the largest mass of their figure, about
two to three times a normal head's share of the body. The head is a satirical
mask: bold, readable, still a real adult face with two eyes, one nose, one
mouth, and two ears. Features may be swollen, pinched, or skewed, but they
stay attached to that one head.

Give every huge head a theatrical expression. Combine two or three visibly
altered features: popped or slit eyes, hooked or flying brows, a megaphone
mouth, a compressed line, inflated cheeks, or flared nostrils. Name the
expression and those features. Adjacent people must contrast. A mild smile,
calm composure, vacant politeness, or generic seriousness is not enough.

The huge head must remain fully visible. Hair, hats, medals, and microphones
may decorate it; they may not replace it or hide the face that carries the
gag.

BODY KIT

Once the head is set, freely compose the rest of each body. For every adult,
independently choose a scale and thickness for each of these regions:

- torso
- chest
- waist
- buttocks and hips
- arms
- hands
- legs
- feet

Each region may be huge, tiny, very long, stubby, thick, or spindly. Name every
region for every adult; do not leave chest, waist, buttocks, hands, or feet
implied by a generic torso. Mix the choices so no two people in the same Theme
share one recipe. Productive clashes include a cathedral head on thread legs, a
puffed chest over a missing waist, log arms ending in coin hands, a vast rear
occupying the only chair, or pillar feet that refuse to leave a balcony.

The kit may violate ordinary proportion. It may not violate human topology.
Every adult keeps exactly one head, one continuous torso, two arms, two hands,
two legs, and two feet. Joints stay attached and bend in a readable direction.
No extra limb, missing limb, detached part, second pelvis, fused person, or
broken joint. Do not grow a penis, extra breast, or extra genital from a
chest, nipple, belly, or back. Nipples stay nipples; a swollen chest is still
a chest. Keep both feet visible. Clothes, sashes, and armor follow the body
volumes instead of correcting them.

Let each chosen volume do the joke's work. Tiny hands cannot hold a seal, a
knife, or a child's-size task claimed as labor. Spindly arms cannot lift the
people or plates they claim to carry. A swollen chest can hide the work being
done behind it. Needle legs cannot stand without a podium, table, partner, or
emblem. A huge rear can occupy the only chair at home, at work, or in office.

CAST

Use exactly the requested number of adult women and exactly the requested
number of adult men. Invent fictional clerks, ministers, guards, donors,

than dressing everyone the same.
Roles may be spouses, partners, peers, hosts, guests, shopkeepers,
performers, colleagues of equal rank, or fictional officials in an invented
office. At erotic and hardcore levels, do not use doctor and patient, teacher
and pupil, boss and subordinate, senior and junior, minister and secretary,

injured anatomy, or a childlike face.

Clothing must read as that nationality's dress, cut to the body kit and to

uniforms, or clothes that contradict the stated nationality.

When a requested gender count is zero, invent no person of that gender. Add no

The place is invented. Do not set the story in a named real capital or sitting
government. The people are Chinese, Japanese, or Korean adults inside that
invented situation.

THE SATIRE

Build every Theme from one visible equation:

    one public claim
    + one private incentive
    + bodies that already tell the truth
    + one content-level action that enacts the claim
    + one emblem object
    = one social fact a viewer can see

Choose a domain. Politics is allowed and should appear sometimes, but it is
not the default. Rotate among:

- political and bureaucratic ritual
- marriage, family, and household labor
- workplace credit and hierarchy
- money, shopping, and status display
- school, exams, and credentials
- medicine, care, and waiting
- food, hosting, and appetite
- media, fame, and looking
- friendship, rivalry, and petty daily friction

The satire is performed, not captioned. Aesthetic gags are performed with
ordinary nonsexual action and costume. Erotic gags are performed with adult
display and non-explicit contact. Hardcore gags are performed with a visible
consensual sexual act. Do not invent a clothed scene and later undress it. If
the content-level action is removed, the joke must collapse.

Cover the lettering and the joke must still survive. Invent fresh situations
instead of recycling one press conference, one kitchen argument, or one
shopping gag with new hats. Useful tensions include ceremony versus labor,
voice versus a mute partner, a seal versus what it claims, care demanded and
care withheld, credit moving away from the work, a feast nobody eats, a
mirror that replaces a person, and a queue that never advances.

The gag is complete only when every requested adult is inside the same action.
A person who merely watches from the side is not in the picture. Do not repeat
the same mechanical joke across Themes: a body used as a desk, a body used as
a chair, or one person feeding another may appear at most once in a run.

CONTACT AND SUPPORT

Every adult must make direct, force-bearing contact with the emblem or with
another adult in the same action. Hands raised in blessing, pointing, or empty
air do not count. Name where each hand, foot, and supporting surface meets.

Impossible proportions still need possible balance. Trace weight from each
body through hips, legs, hands, furniture, or a consenting partner to the
floor. If someone stands, sits, kneels, arches, or lifts, show the counterforce.
Do not stack a standing person over a body on a scale, chair, or table unless
both contact surfaces and the path of weight are obvious. Do not hide a foot
or leave a limb with no job.

PLACE

Set each Theme in one invented room that fits the chosen domain and can host
the requested content-level action: a ministry chamber, plaza, kitchen,
dining corner, shop counter, classroom, clinic, dressing area, livestream
corner, or private room. Keep the place sparse: a few walls, a floor, and the
objects needed for the action. Do not fill it with extra furniture or a city
beyond the room. The place must belong to the action; do not drop an erotic
or sexual act into an unchanged public ceremony.

Give the gag one emblem object that shares the stage with the bodies: a seal,
podium, ledger, cake, phone, scale, diploma, shopping bag, trophy, remote,
chair, or another object born from this joke. The emblem may support a body,
receive a force, or be the thing everyone is pretending not to notice. Keep
at most one small supporting object from the same situation.

If the picture has any writing, every visible character must be English:
uppercase ASCII letters A through Z, with spaces and simple punctuation only.
No Chinese characters, Japanese kana or kanji, Korean hangul, pinyin, bilingual
text, numbers, logos, or gibberish. Lock one short English label of one to
three uppercase words on the emblem or on a sash already worn in the action.
Paint, stamp, stitch, or carve that label into the object. Do not add a
floating caption, speech balloon, subtitle, newspaper column, or extra
placard. If no label is locked, the image contains no writing.

NO NAMED STYLE

Do not name a visual style, medium, genre, or rendering method anywhere in a
Theme or Frame. Do not say photograph, cartoon, comic, illustration, painting,
editorial, cinematic, lithograph, woodcut, silkscreen, hyper-real, or any
similar label. Describe only what is present: people, bodies, clothes,
objects, the room, and the light source as a physical thing in the room.

CONTENT LEVEL

Honor the exact requested content level. Figures, place, and satirical target
stay in force, but the action that carries the satire must change with the
level. The roles are satirical parts worn by consenting fictional adults, not
a workplace or household where refusal is punished.

At aesthetic level, perform the gag with fully opaque costume and nonsexual
action: carrying, stamping, cooking, blocking, applauding, counting, waiting,
serving, shopping, holding a seal, or refusing to move. Reveal the body kit
through cut, stuffing, and strain, never through nudity or sexual contact.

At erotic level, perform the gag with unmistakably adult, non-explicit
sensual display and contact. The social fact must be visible in who is
exposed, who looks, who is looked at, who approaches, who withholds touch,
and who bears the effort of the pose. Every requested adult is inside that
sensual circuit. Do not take an ordinary civic or domestic task and merely
open a garment. A hint of skin on a stamping, cooking, or waiting scene
fails this level, and so does an explicit sexual act.

At erotic level, no penis, testicles, vulva, anus, or semen is visible.
Mouths do not meet breasts, nipples, or genitals. Hands may support a chest;
do not put a mouth on a chest. Do not describe dripping fluid on faces or
breasts. An open megaphone mouth is an expression, not oral sex.

At hardcore level, perform the gag as one clearly visible consensual adult
sexual act already happening. Name the current act and the exact meeting of
bodies so a viewer can place every pelvis, mouth, hand, and support. Position,
weight, and who does the labor are the satirical metaphor. Every requested
adult takes part. Do not write "receiving him," "a standing position," or
"the act occurs" without the contact geometry. Do not leave the main garments
fully on if they hide the act. Use only the existing genitals of each adult:
a man may have one penis, a woman does not grow one from the chest or
elsewhere. Satirize the conduct and the situation, never a body, gender, or
preference as inferior.

No content level permits coercion, minors, intoxication, real-person
likenesses, sex as tribute, payment, medical treatment, or job condition, or
humiliation aimed at identity.

THEME SEED

Each Theme owns one finished cast and one satirical gag. Lock, before any
Frame is made:

- the exact requested adults, names, nationalities, and roles
- each oversize head, exaggerated expression, nationality-coded clothing,
  and complete body kit
- the domain and invented place
- the content-level action that performs the joke
- the emblem
- the social fact
- the optional English label, or the decision that there is no writing

Give the Theme a short natural title. The Theme premise is exactly two
sentences. Sentence one names every requested adult, their nationality,
role, oversize head, exaggerated expression, nationality-coded clothing,
and full body kit. Sentence two names the invented place, the
content-level action, the emblem, and the social fact that action makes
visible. The Theme style is one sentence that only names the room, its
surfaces, and the light source as objects in the scene. It must not name a
medium, genre, or art style.

Do not put camera recipes, discarded alternatives, or style names in the
premise. Do not redesign a figure after the Theme is locked.

FRAME DRAWING

Every Frame is one standalone full-bleed still. It is not a grid, collage,
or split screen. Multiple Frames of one Theme are alternate views of the same
gag with the same figures, not chapters of a plot.

Write every Frame as one unbroken natural-language paragraph. Near the start,
name the invented place and moment. Then describe every adult in visual
order: invented name, age of at least 25, Chinese or Japanese or Korean
nationality, role, oversize head, exaggerated expression with named
features, complete body kit, nationality-coded clothing or remaining
garments, and current held pose. Then the shared content-level action and
the emblem. Name the room and its light only as things in the scene. Do not
name a visual style. The social fact must be readable from that action, not
from a leftover prop. If a label was locked, reproduce it verbatim in
straight double quotation marks on its object and state that it is the only
visible writing, in English. If none was locked, include no writing.

Keep every figure visible from the crown of the huge head through both feet.
Do not crop heads, hands, or feet. Freeze one physically supported instant:
impossible proportions, possible balance. A needle-legged body must lean on
the emblem, another person, or the set. Do not describe a sequence of motions
inside one still. Do not introduce the action with a labeled clause such as
"the content-level action is" or "the hardcore act is"; show it as visible
contact.

DISCARD PILE

Redraw any Theme or Frame that:

- uses the wrong number or gender of people
- adds a crowd or extra human
- gives anyone a normal-sized head
- leaves torso, chest, waist, buttocks, arms, hands, legs, or feet unnamed
- repeats one body kit on two people in the same Theme
- adds, drops, detaches, or fuses a limb or joint
- hides a foot, hand, or the huge head
- gives anyone a mild, generic, or unexaggerated expression
- makes anyone not Chinese, Japanese, or Korean, or dresses them against
  that nationality
- portraits a living public figure, a real sitting government, or a real
  national flag
- aims the joke at identity or nationality rather than conduct
- repeats the same satire domain as the previous Theme
- needs a caption to be understood
- names a visual style, medium, genre, or rendering method
- ignores the requested content level, or tells a joke that would
  survive after its intimate or sexual action is removed
- uses sexual contact or exposure at aesthetic level
- uses only costume, mood, an opened garment on an ordinary task, or implied
  almost-contact at erotic level, or crosses into an explicit sexual act
- shows a penis, extra genital, phallic nipple, or semen at erotic level, or
  grows a genital from a chest, nipple, or back at any level
- uses preparation, aftermath, posing, hidden anatomy, or a nearby ordinary
  scene at hardcore level instead of a current explicit act with readable
  contact geometry that includes every adult
- leaves a hand, foot, or person out of the force chain, or stacks bodies so
  weight has no obvious path to the floor
- uses doctor and patient, boss and subordinate, senior and junior, or sex
  traded for care, work, or money
- mismatches a name to its nationality, or uses bound feet, injury, or a
  childlike face
- changes a locked figure between Frames
- invents writing that was not locked, or drops a locked label
- shows any non-English writing, including Chinese characters, kana, kanji,
  hangul, pinyin, bilingual text, numbers, logos, or gibberish
