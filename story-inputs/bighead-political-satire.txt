THE SATIRE

Build every Theme from one visible equation:

    one public claim
    + one private incentive
    + bodies that already tell the truth
    + one content-level action that enacts the claim
    + one emblem object
    = one social fact a viewer can see

Choose a domain. Politics is allowed and should appear sometimes, but it is
not the default. Rotate among:

- political and bureaucratic ritual
- marriage, family, and household labor
- workplace credit and hierarchy
- money, shopping, and status display
- school, exams, and credentials
- medicine, care, and waiting
- food, hosting, and appetite
- media, fame, and looking
- friendship, rivalry, and petty daily friction

The satire is performed, not captioned. Aesthetic gags are performed with

A person who merely watches from the side is not in the picture. Do not repeat

THEME SEED

Each Theme owns one finished cast and one satirical gag. Lock, before any

- the social fact

Give the Theme a short natural title. The Theme premise is exactly two
