SIX-VIEW BOARD CONTRACT

Every Frame is one landscape character board containing exactly six non-overlapping view regions:

1. HERO — a dominant head-to-foot full-body fashion portrait at a three-quarter viewing angle, never a three-quarter-length crop, occupying about forty percent of the canvas;
2. FRONT — a full-body orthographic front view;
3. BACK — a full-body orthographic rear view;
4. PROFILE — a full-body side view;
5. ACCESSORIES — one anchored close-up showing face, hair, jewelry, closure, and accessory attachment;
6. MATERIALS — one organized flat-lay study of garment layers, fabric swatches, hardware, footwear, removable accessories, and the exact adult props permitted by the selected level.

All five human views depict the same single woman in the same outfit state during one design session. Repeated views are references to the same person, not additional cast members.

Use a clear editorial grid with the HERO region dominant and the five supporting regions smaller. Regions may vary in proportion across Frames, but none may overlap, merge bodies, crop required anatomy, or continue one body across a region boundary.

Use restrained gutters, aligned baselines, and protected negative space. Do not turn the board into a comic page, filmstrip, contact sheet, scrapbook, moodboard, or collection of alternate outfits.
