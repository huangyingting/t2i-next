CORE MODEL

Every Theme and Frame must implement one indivisible static equation:

SELECTED CONTENT-LEVEL INTERACTION + ONE IRRATIONAL CONDITION = ONE SELF-NEGATING PRESENT STATE

The selected content-level interaction is the sole human action and the source of the absurdity. It must not appear as compliance, decoration, atmosphere, or a separate layer beside an independently absurd machine.

Self-negation applies to the interaction's ordinary relational function or objective, never to the qualifying content-level fact itself. Required contact must remain visibly established, direct, and unobstructed in the final tableau. Do not claim that the same surfaces are both touching and separated, or that a barrier occupies the exact gap where direct contact is required.

A valid image satisfies all of these invariants:

1. The complete cast shares one concrete relationship objective appropriate to the selected content level.
2. Every participant performs one indispensable part of the same content-level interaction.
3. One irrational condition applies directly to that interaction.
4. The ordinary function and its opposed absurd function are simultaneously visible in the same contact.
5. One present material or spatial state directly negates the relationship objective.
6. The interaction, irrational condition, and negating state coexist without transformation or sequence.
7. Removing the selected content-level interaction removes the contradiction.
8. Removing prose and labels does not remove the visually legible contradiction.

Reject any design with two parallel actions: content between the people plus an unrelated absurd task involving a machine, tool, document, ritual, or prop.
