BRIEF

Create photorealistic Themes and Frames about consenting adult intimacy in the
near and medium future. Technology has changed bodies, distance, reproduction,
labor, privacy, and sexual positions, while daily life remains materially
ordinary. Every image should feel like an unsentimental photograph made inside
a real home, habitat, hotel, clinic, workshop, transport cabin, or rented room.

The governing style is cold lived-in realism: advanced systems are bought,
rented, repaired, cleaned, charged, patched, and argued over. The future must
be unmistakable at first glance through architecture, materials, light, and
body-scale technology, not through spectacle or a single gadget in a present-
day room.

PRIORITY AND OUTPUT CONTRACT

Apply requirements in this order:

1. exact requested cast, adulthood, consent, and coherent anatomy;
2. image-legible sexual action at the requested content level;
3. one active speculative technology physically changing that action;
4. physically credible support, contact, and force geometry;
5. coherent future world, cold light, environment, and camera;
6. variation, maintenance history, cost, privacy, and emotional tension.

Theme titles, premises, styles, and Frame prose use grammatical English and
printable ASCII U+0020 through U+007E only. Replace curly quotes, long dashes,
accented letters, mathematical symbols, and all other non-ASCII characters.

Every Frame is one natural English paragraph intended for a text-to-image
model. Write only positive visible instructions. Never copy a rule, rejection,
warning, checklist, alternative, or negative-prompt phrase into Frame prose.
Do not write meta sentences such as this is not, do not show, avoid, reject,
without, no duplicate, or no extra. Silently use those checks before returning,
then describe only the accepted visible composition.

Show blank, dark, oblique, or icon-only screens. Keep packaging unbranded and
surfaces free of writing. The image contains no captions, subtitles, logos,
watermarks, timestamps, product names, interface paragraphs, pseudo-writing,
letters, numbers, QR codes, barcodes, or readable signs.

Target 220-280 words and five to seven sentences per Frame. The absolute limits
are 320 words and 2100 ASCII characters, including complex casts and support
systems. Count silently and rewrite any longer draft by removing secondary
props, repeated mechanics, and explanatory clauses. Mention each person,
device, light, anchor, and problem once. Put cast, action, anatomy, and
technology in the first half.

Every biological human has one coherent head, torso, pelvis, two arms, two

FUTURE VISUAL SYSTEM

Choose exactly one primary speculative development per Theme. Supporting
architecture, furniture, utilities, and ordinary products share a plausible
future baseline but do not introduce another narrative invention.

The selected technology answers four visual questions:

1. what ordinary need caused its use;
2. where it attaches, charges, vents, folds, drains, or bears weight;
3. what proves repeated ownership, rental, cleaning, repair, or wear;
4. what emotional, economic, privacy, or practical problem remains unsolved.

Build future information at three simultaneous scales:

- room scale: one large functional structure crosses much of the composition,
  such as an emitter lattice, ceiling rail, environmental spine, pressure-shell
  ribs, adaptive floor, heat-exchange wall, or robotic furniture gantry;
- body scale: the technology supports, covers, projects, replaces, senses, or
  transforms a substantial part of at least one participant;
- contact scale: sexual contact visibly compresses light, bends material,
  changes pressure cells, moves actuators, shifts thermal color, or alters a
  responsive surface.

Choose one coherent visual language:

1. Retrofitted megacity domestic: compact composite panels, integrated service
   ribs, aging privacy glass, modular furniture, dense vertical storage.
2. Brutalist habitat utility: curved pressure shells, graphite ribs, ventilation
   manifolds, magnetic rails, acoustic blankets, ceramic floor plates.
3. Biotech household realism: translucent washable polymers, sensor fabrics,
   gel components, sterilization recesses, plant-based composites.
4. Degraded future hospitality: worn emitter tracks, programmable partitions,

6. Climate-adapted interior: heat-exchange walls, condensate channels, filtered

7. Lunar or Martian domestic industry: pressure shells, abrasion seals, dust
   vestibules, fold-flat furniture, cargo nets, reused shipping modules.
8. Spectral telepresence room: emitter rails, depth sensors, a single colored
   light body, contact compression, processor cooling, localized color spill.

Include at least six coherent future signals distributed across architecture,
furniture, lighting, and background objects. Use matte ceramic composite,

Future visual intensity comes from functional scale, material contrast, spatial

used environment rather than cyberpunk, space opera, fantasy art, glossy
advertising, a game cinematic, an immaculate laboratory, or a luxury showroom.

PHOTOGRAPHIC SYSTEM

Use a medium-wide complete-scene photograph with a rectilinear 35-50 mm
equivalent lens and f/5.6-f/11 depth of field. Show complete bodies whenever
the position permits and retain the room boundaries, active technology, load
path, and environmental context. Close-up, macro, face-dominant, shallow-focus,
fisheye, and ultra-wide compositions fail the brief.

Organize foreground, middle ground, and background. Give each plane at least
two readable objects or structures in separate silhouette slots. Keep limbs,
hands, cables, supports, and props separated by clean negative space. Nothing
important merges into a body or blocks the primary contact.

Use at least one motivated 5200K-6500K architectural or utility key. State its
position, direction, hardness, falloff, and effect on skin, sweat, condensation,
translucent material, and room depth. Functional device accents remain small
and subordinate.

The palette is cool dominant: graphite, cold silver, ice blue, cyan, teal,
desaturated jade, blue-green, muted violet, and lunar white. Human skin remains
natural and slightly warmer than the room for separation, with neutral
highlights and blue-grey or violet shadows. Warm bedside lamps, tungsten,
sunset orange, sodium yellow, amber grading, bronze skin, and golden highlights
never dominate.

Choose six concise reality anchors from different categories:

- household: rumpled bedding, laundry, towels, toiletries, storage, food;
- infrastructure: vents, sockets, filters, gutters, seals, pipes, batteries;
- maintenance: patches, scuffs, replacement screws, cleaned seams, worn pads;
- economics: rental case, reused fabric, mismatched module, metered resource;
- privacy: thin partition, corridor light, shared wall, narrow enclosure;
- body: sweat, pressure mark, condensation, adhesive trace, fatigue.

Atmosphere is physical: humid air, cooling draft, condensation, dust at seals,
vibration, recycled-air dryness, fabric movement, corridor noise, or limited
privacy. Show one unresolved emotion through gaze, jaw, brow, breath, grip, or
posture rather than explaining it.
