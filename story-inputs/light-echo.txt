BRIEF

Create atmospheric light-echo Themes where one practical light source produces
a sequence of secondary illuminations across a real environment. The requested
adult cast occupies the final illuminated zone and responds to the source's
physical behavior.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
inside the final illuminated zone. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, clearly visible face and body, position, gaze, and necessary action.
A shadow, reflection, portrait, or implied off-camera recipient does not count.
Additional CLI-requested adults may appear, but she must remain the human payoff
of the light path. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

LIGHT CHAIN

Build the path as exactly five named positions: original source -> solid or
liquid link 1 -> solid or liquid link 2 -> solid or liquid link 3 -> final cast
zone. Volumetric dust, smoke, or mist may reveal an existing segment but never
counts as one of the three links. Use credible combinations of water, glass,
polished metal, pale fabric, prisms, perforated screens, glossy tile, or
architectural reflectors. Every link must have line of sight to the previous one
and must send a clearly directed, weaker outgoing beam toward the next.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, time, exact location, adult cast, practical source,
link 1, link 2, link 3, final light pattern, and the choice or discovery it
reveals, in that order. The style sentence defines exposure, contrast, color
separation, atmosphere, and medium.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame shows all five named positions in one coherent view. For link 1,
link 2, and link 3, explicitly describe incident direction, receiving surface,
outgoing direction, color or shape change, and decreasing relative intensity.
The cast
has one clear action in the final zone; faces, hands, and body edges remain
readable without unrelated fill light.

Maintain plausible inverse-square falloff, reflection angles, refraction,
shadow direction, volumetric visibility, and material roughness. Do not turn
every surface into a mirror, duplicate people in reflections, or use impossible
light beams visible in clean air.

VARIETY AND REJECTION

Vary source, optical chain, architecture, palette, camera axis, and human stakes.
Reject generic colored lighting, arbitrary glowing objects, a chain with missing
line of sight, or prose that lists effects without tracing the light path.
