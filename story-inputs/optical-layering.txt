BRIEF

Create richly composed optical-layering Themes photographed through one
transparent or perforated foreground material. Each of three depth layers contributes distinct information
about the requested adult cast and setting while preserving one spatially
coherent image.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
as the clearly visible subject in Layer 2. Give her a specific adult age, stable
appearance, unobstructed face, owned body, support, gaze, and necessary action.
A reflection, portrait, silhouette, filtered duplicate, or off-camera person
does not count. Additional CLI-requested adults may share Layer 2, but she must
remain legible through Layer 1. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman
cast.

LAYER SYSTEM

Use the exact CLI-requested cast and no additional people, assistants, reflected
figures, portraits, or human images. Give each person only one identity and one
name.

Use exactly three depth layers in camera-to-background order: Layer 1 is one
transparent or perforated foreground filter; Layer 2 is open air containing the
complete cast and action entirely behind Layer 1; Layer 3 is one opaque
background anchor entirely behind the cast. Choose
materials such as rain-streaked
glass, mesh, lace, leaves, translucent paper, perforated metal, or scratched
acrylic only when physically suitable. Do not use steam, smoke, mist, or vapor
as a layer. Do not introduce a second filter or separator.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise names country, exact site, adult cast, the contents of all three
layers in camera-to-background order, and current action. No extra pane, filter, screen,
reflection, or intervening plane may be introduced. The style sentence defines
lens, aperture strategy, palette, light, and material rendering.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame is one exposure from one camera in front of Layer 1. Describe the
three layers in order,
their distance relationships and focus behavior. Keep at least one face, every body's ownership, the decisive
action, and all support points unambiguous.
Focus on the cast in Layer 2. Layer 1 may be softly out of focus and Layer 3
slightly softer. Keep the background behind every part of the cast.

Occlusion must have purpose. A layer may soften or partly conceal but cannot
erase cast count, create extra limbs, hide essential contact, or become a vague
foreground smear. Reflections may show only light and architecture, never a
second person. Condensation, droplets, shadows, and perforations follow gravity,
focus, and source direction.

Reject arbitrary things held before the lens, four filters with no narrative
function, total haze, mirrored duplication, or prose that describes each layer
as a separate picture.
