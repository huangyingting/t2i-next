BRIEF

Create inventive forced-perspective photography Themes in which ordinary objects
and the requested adult cast align across real depth to produce one impossible
but camera-made relationship. The effect must be achievable in one exposure
without compositing, duplicated people, or scale-changing anatomy.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
as a clearly visible participant in the forced-perspective illusion. Give her a
specific adult age, stable appearance, real depth plane, grounded position,
gaze, and necessary action. A silhouette, portrait, reflection, miniature
figurine, or off-camera person does not count. Additional CLI-requested adults
may appear without replacing her role. This brief is incompatible with a
zero-woman cast.

ILLUSION LOGIC

For each Theme choose a new location, near object, far subject, alignment, and
human purpose. The near object must alter the apparent function, scale, or
contact of the far subject from the selected camera position. A hand touching a
landmark or a person appearing inside a cup is too familiar unless transformed
by a stronger practical action and material consequence.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise states country, exact site, cast, depth-separated elements, resolved
illusion, and the current action that makes the trick meaningful. The style
sentence defines lens range, depth-of-field strategy, palette, and light needed
to bind the planes.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame is one full-bleed resolved composition. Specify camera height and
direction, foreground object's support, near/middle/far plane distances in
qualitative terms, alignment edges, and cast positions. Preserve subtle depth
cues so the scene rewards a second look while reading instantly as one image.

All bodies remain ordinary in size and anatomy. Contact exists only where
surfaces truly meet; apparent contact across depth must not cause dents, shadows,
or force transfer. Shadows, focus, haze, and perspective must follow actual
distance. No split screens, miniatures presented as real people, floating props,
mirrors, or post-production explanations.

Reject tired tourist gestures, effects dependent on hidden extra people, or any
Frame where the prose claims an illusion without describing the exact alignment
that produces it.
