BRIEF

Create polished cinematic Themes in which one room contains a large anamorphic
image that resolves only from one exact camera position. The requested adult
cast must physically inhabit the room while the illusion and the ordinary
architecture remain simultaneously understandable.

WOMAN CAST GATE

Every Theme and every Frame must include at least one unmistakably adult woman
as an active, clearly visible subject. She must have a specific adult age,
appearance, position, gaze, and necessary action. Never replace her with a man,
an androgynous silhouette, a reflected figure, a portrait, or an implied
off-camera presence. If the CLI requests additional adults, include them without
weakening her visual role. This brief is incompatible with a zero-woman cast.

THE VISUAL ENGINE

For each Theme, invent a different place, subject, and reason for the anamorphic
construction. Build the resolved image from paint, tape, fabric, light, objects,
or architectural color distributed across several depths. It must not be a flat
mural. From the hero viewpoint, separated fragments align into one crisp symbol,
creature, landscape, or impossible opening; elsewhere, they visibly break across
floor, walls, furniture, and suspended elements.

THEME CONTRACT

The premise must name the country, exact room function, adult cast, resolved
image, fragmented materials, three separated depth planes, and the human
situation that makes this instant matter. Its first sentence establishes the
room and cast; its second sentence uses the explicit pattern "fragments on
[near plane], [middle plane], and [far plane] align as [resolved image] because
[current human purpose]." The style sentence must state the photographic
medium, palette, and the contrast between the resolved illusion and the
uncorrected room.

FRAME CONTRACT

Each Frame is one full-bleed still, not a diagram or multi-panel comparison.
Choose either the exact resolving viewpoint or a nearby revealing viewpoint.
When resolved, preserve sharp oblique edges and depth cues to prove the image
occupies real space. When revealed, keep enough alignment to let the viewer
mentally reconstruct the intended image. Give every requested person one clear
position, gaze target, and action that interacts with the fragmented installation
without blocking its essential alignment.

Use a plausible 28-70 mm lens, one coherent horizon, traceable architecture,
contact shadows, and depth-appropriate focus. Describe which fragment sits on
which physical surface and how at least three separated depths align. No floating
paint, impossible reflections, duplicated people, explanatory labels, arrows,
captions, or visible construction notes.

VARIETY AND REJECTION

Across Themes vary room type, resolved subject, construction method, scale,
viewpoint height, palette, and cast relationship. Reject any Theme that could be
described as a person standing before a mural, any Frame where the illusion is
only asserted, or any composition whose fragments do not have precise physical
supports. For all three planes, name one exact contour fragment and the matching
contours it joins in the other planes; do not rely on the phrase "they align."
Keep traceable room edges sharp enough to reveal the depth construction.
