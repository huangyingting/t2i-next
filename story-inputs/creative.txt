BRIEF

Create varied, award-caliber, hyper-realistic six-panel campaign concept boards.
Each Theme must be a fresh visual invention, not a variation on a prescribed
subject. The board contains one recurring miniature adult cast and one colossal
hero ecology whose real properties make six impossible but instantly legible
images possible.

This is explicitly a non-narrative high-concept campaign board. Conceptual
invention replaces conventional plot, relationship conflict, or a person merely
exploring a setting.

OPEN SUBJECT SELECTION

Choose subjects freely and unpredictably. A subject may come from any domain,
including domains not suggested here. Do not assign categories, moods, objects,
materials, or intensities by theme_id. Theme IDs identify outputs only.

Across requested Themes, maximize distance in subject, world logic, material
behavior, human activity, palette, and visual rhythm. Do not choose several
subjects that all become caves, forests, mazes, baths, beds, shelters, or
landscapes.

THE CONCEPT EQUATION

Build each concept with this equation:

    familiar hero + distant system or ritual + truthful material mechanism
    = one surprising campaign proposition

The distant system or ritual must change what the hero does and what the cast
can do with it. Scale by itself is not an idea.

The hero is an ecology, not just one enlarged object. Use several of its real
components, stages, states, byproducts, tools, packaging forms, or effects. They
must remain recognizable while acquiring useful new roles inside one coherent
world.

A strong proposition completes this sentence with concrete, visible nouns and
verbs:

    This is not merely a giant [hero]. Its [real property or component] becomes
    [unexpected function], so the miniature cast can [specific desire, ritual,
    service, game, contradiction, or transformation].

Reject the proposition if the final clause is only "explore it," "feel it,"
"connect with it," "be immersed in it," or another vague sensory treatment.

SECOND-ORDER LEAP

Before writing, generate multiple unrelated candidates and select the one with
the clearest second-order leap. The final idea must combine at least three
compatible creative operations, chosen for the selected subject rather than by
quota:

- function transfer: a real component becomes transport, fashion, furniture,
  architecture, machinery, service infrastructure, stagecraft, sport, or
  another unexpected working role;
- state drama: different physical states create different actions and images;
- miniature logistics: the cast builds a working tool, route, vehicle, rig,
  workshop, delivery system, or civic system from the hero;
- scale crossing: a hand, tool, flow, mechanism, shadow, or force from another
  scale intervenes and changes the micro-world;
- character shift: a component acts as host, companion, rival, audience,
  performer, guide, or creature without turning cartoonish;
- material weather: a truthful byproduct becomes climate, light, atmosphere,
  precipitation, or environmental force;
- ritual or game: an ordinary use becomes a ceremony, courtship rule, sport,
  feast, performance, challenge, or elegant practical joke;
- hero reveal: dispersed components resolve into an iconic finished form,
  service moment, presentation, or other visual payoff.

These are open creative operations, not Theme assignments or fixed panel roles.
Invent other operations when they serve the proposition better.

THE SIX-STUNT TEST

Invent more than six visual stunts, then retain the six strongest. Every retained
stunt must:

- work as a standalone campaign key visual;
- use a different role, state, process, component, scale, or physical force;
- show the adult cast doing something necessary to the proposition;
- contain clear contact, force, response, and material consequence when bodies
  or tools interact with the hero;
- remain understandable without a caption; and
- belong unmistakably to the same hero ecology.

Most stunts must change function or state, not merely change camera angle or
body pose. At least one stunt must contain a category-breaking event such as a
working miniature system, an outside-scale intervention, material weather, a
component with a social role, or an equally strong invention arising naturally
from the hero.

HARD REJECTION GATE

Discard and replace the entire Theme when four or more stunts can be summarized
with passive bodily verbs such as walk through, stand beside, gaze at, touch,
stroke, lean on, lie on, bathe in, embrace, or climb.

The following are beginnings, not finished concepts:

- repeated long things becoming a forest;
- lines or fabric becoming a maze;
- liquid becoming a bath;
- a hollow object becoming a cave or room;
- a curved surface becoming a bed or slide;
- a mushroom, shell, leaf, or cloth becoming shelter;
- a large device merely being operated at scale.

One of these forms may appear only if another idea overturns it through changed
function, ritual, law, social meaning, logistics, or scale. Sensual photography
cannot compensate for a weak proposition.

THEME-STAGE OUTPUT CONTRACT

Use the Theme schema normally, but make the two-sentence premise carry an
auditable creative plan:

1. Sentence one states the hero, the distant system or ritual, and the physical
   mechanism that creates the second-order conceptual leap.
2. Sentence two contains exactly six concise visual-stunt seeds separated by
   semicolons. Each seed names the transformed hero component or state, its new
   function, and a concrete cast action.

Do not spend the premise on generic mood, intimacy, exploration, or backstory.
The style field carries medium, palette, light, material rendering, and visual
rhythm. The six seeds may use any order; no panel position has a permanently
assigned narrative function.

FRAME-STAGE OUTPUT CONTRACT

Produce one portrait 2-column by 3-row grid per Theme. It is one image with six
cleanly separated regions and no visible title, caption, label, typography,
logo, watermark, border text, or interface element.

Realize all six stunt seeds from the Theme premise, one per region. Do not
replace, merge, or dilute them. Explicitly describe Region 1 through Region 6;
a general board synopsis without six expanded region descriptions is invalid.
When frames_per_theme is 1, the single Narrative Frame is the entire six-region
board, not one region or a summary. Use roughly 450 to 650 words, or equivalent
detail in the requested language. Expand each seed into a photographic scene
with:

- the recurring cast's exact action and leverage;
- the hero component's scale, role, and physical state;
- visible cause and effect;
- tactile deformation, displacement, spray, tension, fracture, compression,
  flow, reflection, residue, or another truthful material response;
- composition, camera distance and angle, lens character, focus, lighting, and
  color; and
- enough surrounding context to make both scale and concept immediately clear.

The six regions form a flexible visual rhythm, not six equal poses and not a
mandatory beginning-middle-climax sequence. Any region may be quiet, comic,
intimate, kinetic, monumental, or revelatory when the chosen concept calls for
it.

CAST AND CONTINUITY

Use exactly the requested number of adult women and adult men. All depicted
people are clearly adults. The same cast recurs across the six regions; repeated
views do not add people. Give each person a stable compact identity: apparent
age, ancestry or complexion, body build, face structure, and hair.

Preserve identity, body details, styling logic, and any transformed wardrobe or
body marks across all regions unless the proposition visibly changes them.
Supporting tools must be built from or materially linked to the hero ecology,
not added as arbitrary spectacle.

CONTENT LEVEL

Interpret the chosen concept specifically through the requested content_level.
Do not let content intensity choose or limit the subject.

- aesthetic: elegant, non-explicit, publication-safe body awareness and
  interaction;
- erotic: clearly adult sensual charge, intentional exposure or concealment,
  intimate self-touch where conceptually useful, and strong physical tension
  without explicit sexual acts;
- hardcore: clearly adult explicit sexual action may be central when requested,
  while anatomy, contact, agency, physical plausibility, and the high-concept
  campaign proposition remain precise.

Do not sanitize erotic or hardcore into neutral wellness imagery. Do not add
sexual content beyond the selected level.

PHOTOGRAPHIC STANDARD

Render the impossible as premium practical photography: believable gravity,
scale, optics, material microtexture, skin texture, moisture, fibers, particles,
reflections, shadows, and contact deformation. Favor one controlled visual
language with enough contrast between regions to keep the grid alive. Avoid
generic fantasy haze, decorative clutter, cartoon physics, empty spectacle, and
six near-identical camera views.

FINAL SILENT CHECK

Before returning output, verify:

- the subject was freely invented and not inferred from theme_id;
- the premise follows the exact two-sentence contract;
- sentence two contains six semicolon-separated, visibly different stunts;
- at least three creative operations combine into one coherent proposition;
- the hero appears through multiple truthful components, states, or effects;
- most cast actions are active and consequential rather than passive contact;
- all six stunt seeds are realized in the Frame;
- the requested cast count and content level are exact; and
- the final grid contains no rendered text.

If any check fails, revise before returning.
